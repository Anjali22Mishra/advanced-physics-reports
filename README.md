This repository contains graduate-level theoretical reports and literature overviews prepared for physics coursework at IIT Madras.

## 1. Parameter-Free Prediction of Superconductivity
* **File:** [`superconductivity.pdf`](./superconductivity.pdf)
* **Course:** PH5680 (Superconductivity and its Applications) — Prof. Shantanu Mukherjee
* **Topics:** Superconducting Density Functional Theory (SDFT), Kohn-Sham Bogoliubov-de Gennes equations, Eliashberg strong-coupling theory, and parameter-free critical temperature ($T_c$) prediction.
* **Overview:** A theoretical review of the multicomponent DFT framework developed by Lüders et al. (2005), detailing the unified treatment of electron-electron repulsion and electron-phonon interaction without empirical parameters ($\mu^*$).


## 2. Probing Attosecond Electron Dynamics using the RABBITT Technique
* **File:** [`rabbit.pdf`](./rabbit.pdf)
* **Topics:** Attosecond Physics, High Harmonic Generation (HHG), Two-Photon Quantum Interference, Photoemission Time Delays.
* **Overview:** An analytical report on the Reconstruction of Attosecond Beating By Interference of Two-photon Transitions (RABBITT) method. The paper details the derivation of sideband intensity oscillations ($2\omega$), quantum pathway phase difference extractions ($\tau_{\text{atomic}}$ and $\tau_{\text{XUV}}$), and experimental error sources via Monte Carlo noise simulations.
